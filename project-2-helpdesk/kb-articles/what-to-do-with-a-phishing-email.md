# What to do with a phishing email

**Applies to:** all Firma GmbH staff.

## What is phishing?

Phishing is a fake email (or text message) that tries to trick you into clicking a link, opening a file or giving away your password. It often pretends to come from IT, your bank, a delivery company or your manager.

## How to spot it

Be careful when an email:

- asks for your **password**, codes or personal data. IT will never ask for these
- creates **pressure**: "Act within 24 hours or your account will be closed"
- has a **strange sender address**. The name looks right, but the address behind it does not match the company
- has **spelling mistakes** or odd wording
- contains **unexpected attachments** or links. Hover your mouse over a link, without clicking, to see where it really goes
- is **unexpected**, even if it looks like a colleague wrote it

## If you receive a suspicious email

1. **Do not click** any link and **do not open** any attachment.
2. **Do not reply** and do not call numbers from the email.
3. **Report it** to the IT help desk. Use "Report phishing" in your email program if you see that button, or forward the email to the help desk as an attachment.
4. **Delete it** after you have reported it.

## If you already clicked or entered your password

Do not worry, and do not hide it. Quick reporting really helps.

1. **Contact the help desk immediately** and say what you clicked or entered.
2. **Disconnect from the network** if you opened an attachment (unplug the cable or turn Wi-Fi off), but keep the PC on.
3. **Change your password** from another safe device, as soon as the help desk tells you to.
4. Do not use the same password anywhere else.

## Remember

- It is always better to ask than to click.
- Reporting a harmless email is fine. We would rather check ten safe emails than miss one dangerous one.
