# How to reset or change your password

**Applies to:** all Firma GmbH staff who sign in to a company PC.

## Quick answer

- If you **remember** your password and want to change it, follow "Change your password" below.
- If you **forgot** it, or your account is **locked**, contact the IT help desk. We will reset it for you.

## Change your password (you know the current one)

1. Press **Ctrl + Alt + Delete**.
2. Choose **Change a password**.
3. Enter your current password, then your new password twice.
4. Press **Enter**.

## Rules for a good password

Your new password must:

- be at least 8 characters long (longer is better)
- include upper and lower case letters
- include at least one number or symbol
- not contain your name or username
- be different from your recent passwords

Tip: use a short sentence, for example three or four random words with a number. It is easy to remember and hard to guess.

## If your password has expired

Windows will ask you to choose a new password when you sign in. Follow the steps on the screen. If your new password is rejected, check the rules above.

## If your account is locked

Too many wrong attempts lock the account for a short time. Do not keep trying. Wait a few minutes, or call the help desk. Check that no old device, such as your phone's email app, still uses the old password.

## Contact the help desk when

- you forgot your password
- your account is locked and does not unlock
- the new password is rejected and you do not know why

## Please never

- share your password, not even with IT. We will never ask for it by email or phone
- write it on a sticky note
- use the same password for work and private accounts
