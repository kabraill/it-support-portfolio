## Summary

The second monitor flickers every few minutes and sometimes goes black for one second. The cable is plugged in tightly. The first monitor is fine.

## Troubleshooting steps

1. **Check the cable and ports.** Unplug and replug the cable at both ends. Try another cable, and another port on the PC or docking station.
2. **Swap the monitors.** Does the flicker follow the monitor or the port? This tells you whether the monitor or the PC is at fault.
3. **Check the refresh rate and resolution** (Display settings → Advanced display). Set the refresh rate to the monitor's recommended value (for example 60 Hz).
4. **Check the graphics driver.** Is it up to date? Roll back if the problem started after an update.
5. **Check for interference and power.** Keep power cables away from the video cable, and try another socket.

## Root cause

*Assumed for this scenario:* a damaged or poor-quality video cable. With a new cable, the flicker stopped on the same monitor and port.

## Resolution

1. Replace the cable with a new one of the same type.
2. Set the monitor to its recommended resolution and refresh rate.
3. Watch the screen for 15 minutes with the user, and ask the user to report if the flicker comes back.

## Prevention

- Keep spare cables, and buy quality cables for new workstations.
